## What the app will and will not claim

- All demo documents are generated synthetic PDFs/CSVs with a visible `SYNTHETIC – DEMO` watermark. Synthetic ISINs use country code `ZZ`, which is not an ISO 3166 code, so they can never collide with a real instrument; the validator labels them "synthetic".
- The app does not fetch any external fund data at runtime. Product fees in the demo come from synthetic KID PDFs uploaded alongside the bank documents.
- Any number shown in the dashboard is computed by the engine from extracted facts and links to a page and rectangle in the source file.
