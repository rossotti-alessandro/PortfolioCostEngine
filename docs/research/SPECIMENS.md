# Specimen corpus: real public documents

399 real, public PDFs from 289 institutions, used to test FeeLens extraction against real layouts
instead of synthetic ones. Collected 2026-11-06 (story SC-2.3) in three waves, by 15 search agents with one
document family each.

- **Which documents a client has, and which are public:** [CLIENT_DOCUMENTS.md](CLIENT_DOCUMENTS.md).
- **Coverage (type × language × institution):** [`data/specimens/COVERAGE.md`](../../data/specimens/COVERAGE.md),
  generated by `python3 data/specimens/tools/coverage.py`.
- **Manifest:** [`data/reference/specimen_corpus.csv`](../../data/reference/specimen_corpus.csv), one row per
  file: source URL, SHA-256, retrieval date, issuer, taxonomy code, language, jurisdiction, ISINs, licence and
  robots note, `expected` values and their source, category, title, document date, pages, encryption and
  scan flags, product family. The first 11 columns match `data/reference/public_corpus.csv` (demo thread).
  It lives in `data/reference/` because the pre-commit hook only accepts CSVs there.
- **The PDFs are not in git** (third-party copyright). `python3 data/specimens/tools/fetch.py` (stdlib only)
  downloads them into the git-ignored `var/specimens/` (about 240 MB) and checks every SHA-256. `--check`
  verifies without network.
- **Pipeline run:** `cd backend && uv run python ../data/specimens/tools/evaluate.py` calls
  `pce.extraction.parsers.extract` (the entry point the upload API uses) on every file, applies the API's
  25 MB limit and review rule, and writes [`data/specimens/RESULTS.md`](../../data/specimens/RESULTS.md)
  (committed) and `artifacts/specimens/results.json` (git-ignored; contains snippets).

## How each file was validated

Starts with `%PDF`; `pdfinfo` opens it; `pdftotext` on pages 1-3 returns at least 200 characters that name
the issuer (and the ISIN for product documents), except files flagged `scanned`; the document is what the row
claims; SHA-256 recorded and duplicates dropped across all slices. Document dates come from the text; a
month-only date gets day 01, and a missing date stays empty with the clue in `notes`. At most two files per
issuer and language were kept unless the layouts differ, to favour template variety over volume.

## Access rules followed

- robots.txt checked for every host, including AI-crawler rules; the result is recorded per row. No login,
  paywall, CAPTCHA, WAF challenge, 401/403 or disclaimer gate was bypassed, and no browser was spoofed.
- Never touched: ubs.com, credit-suisse.com, bancastato.ch, swissfunddata.ch. UBS-managed or UBS-issued
  documents found on third-party hosts were dropped (a UBS ETF KID, Bordier funds run by UBS Fund Management).
- Dropped after collection: 8 structured-product KIDs under bnl.it `/content/dam/rsc/kid-priips/`
  (robots disallows `/rsc/kid-priips/`; the letter allows the longer path, the intent does not), one
  distributor form naming a real broker officer, one exact duplicate.
- Skipped for robots: all Raiffeisen local-bank tariff PDFs (`*dam/www/*.pdf$`), comdirect, BIL, Andbank,
  Banca Popolare di Sondrio (Suisse), Clientis, Nidwaldner KB (blocks AI crawlers), avl-investmentfonds
  (blocks AI crawlers), derinet.vontobel.ch, products.swisscanto.com, kid.baloise.lu, DZ Bank `/bib/`,
  fundinfo, vwdservices, janushenderson docs, comgest fund docs, borsaitaliana `/media/*.pdf`, grawe.it.
- Skipped for 401/403/WAF: Pictet and Vontobel AM sites, Fidelity International, Leonteq, Raiffeisen
  structured products, Robeco files, Mediobanca certificates, Banca Mediolanum, Clartan, flatex.de and others.
- **Open decision for Ale:** the two Raiffeisen Locarno URLs have no `.pdf` suffix, so raiffeisen.ch's
  robots rules match them by intent but not by letter. The demo thread uses the German one. They stay in
  until Ale decides.
- Many KIDs come from third-party document hosts (docs.extraetf.com, assets.finanzfluss.de, fsl.lv1871.de,
  api.publifund.com, api.kneip.com, prepar-vie.fr, data.payoff.ch, static.milanofinanza.it). Each row says so.

## Re-download

The first 72 rows were re-downloaded into an empty folder: 70/72 hashes matched. UniCredit answered 403 to the
script's user agent (curl had worked) and ING-DiBa closed the connection; neither was retried with another
user agent. Publishers update KIDs monthly and price lists yearly, so `CHANGED` rows are normal over time; the
Xetra-Gold KID is regenerated on every download and will always report `CHANGED`.

Every remaining KID ongoing-cost miss is a missing value; no wrong value was found. Three early
"disagreements" came from the reference extractor, not the parser, and were corrected after reading the page.

Where it works: fund, ETF and structured-product KIDs are classified 160/161, and most give their ongoing
cost. Where it does not yet: factsheets (1 fact in 42; no parser), insurance KIDs (3 of 30 give a fact), filled
client specimens (3 of 17; the 4 best UK/US sample statements give nothing), prospectuses (page limit), and
tariffs (34 of 81 give facts). Public filled specimens with real figures barely exist in DE/IT/CH: banks there
publish reading guides or images, so statement testing needs the synthetic plan in CLIENT_DOCUMENTS.md §3.

**Honest caveats.**

- "Inside the page" is a sanity check of the bounding box, not proof that it covers the right figure.
- Only 34 values were read on the page by Claude or the demo thread; no human has re-checked them.
  The 104 `pdftotext-regex` values measure agreement between two extractors, not truth.
- **Tariff precision is the main open risk.** In the first spot check, tariff facts had wrong fee codes or
  were not fees at all. The demo thread fixed most of them (16d5225), but ZKB brokerage tiers are still
  labelled as custody and multi-column tables keep column 1 without its header. Tariff facts must stay
  "needs review" until section headings drive the fee code reliably.
- The bug list for each run was sent to the demo thread, which owns the parsers.
