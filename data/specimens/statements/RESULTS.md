# Holdings and AUM on real public statements — results

Generated by `data/specimens/statements/score.py` (SC-9.6) against `gold.json`, 2026-11-12, on `main` plus the
two fixes of this story. Run from `backend/`: `uv run python ../data/specimens/statements/score.py`.

## Why this exists

Holdings and AUM drive every percentage in a client report, but until now they were measured only on the
two synthetic prospects. The arena's 899 hand-checked fees contain 9 from statements and no holdings.

## How far to trust this

Four usable public statements from four institutions (US, UK x2, Canada); 34 holdings checked one by one
and 13 account totals. Most public "sample statements" are reading guides with annotated or blank pages
(BIL, Basel-Stadt, Scotia, SGKB, ZKB): no holdings to check. The 30+ page target of SC-9.6 is not reachable
from public material alone; Swiss and Italian private-bank statements are not published. The supervised
pilot (`docs/READINESS.md` section 4) is where holdings accuracy on real Swiss statements gets measured.
