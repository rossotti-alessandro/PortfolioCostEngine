> **One-liner**: Each person gets their own account and role from an admin page, every request is checked against one permission table, sessions end on sign-out, idle time or revocation, and a report is approved by a second person.

## Description

### Context

Audit of 2026-11-25 (thread "Rebuild CRM for mobile and browser"):
- One env-seeded admin holding every role (PR #9); no way to add colleagues or tell who did what.
- Roles: analyst, senior, relationship_manager. No admin (user management) and no read-only (auditor) role.
- Authorisation is per route and partial: internal economics and approval are gated, every other write
  (CRM, uploads, corrections) is open to any role.
- Sessions are stateless cookies valid 12 h: sign-out only deletes the cookie, a copied token stays valid;
  no idle timeout; no way to revoke one person.
- Approval needs the senior role but the same person may prepare and approve (no four-eyes).

### User Flow Impact

- Admin: new "Team & access" page: add a person (name, roles, one-time password shown once), change roles,
  deactivate, reset password, sign a person out everywhere.
- Everyone: first sign-in with a one-time password asks for a new password; sessions end after 30 minutes
  without activity; "Sign out" really ends the session.
- Viewer (read-only / compliance): sees everything except internal economics, cannot change anything.
- Approving a client report: refused for the person who uploaded documents or corrected values on it
  ("a second person must approve"). `PCE_FOUR_EYES=0` turns it off for a one-person demo.

### Performance / Efficiency Impact

One indexed session lookup per request; last-seen written at most once a minute per session.

### Proposed Approach

1. `db.py`: new tables `user_account`, `auth_session` (no change to existing tables).
2. `permissions.py`: one table role -> permissions; `required(method, path)`; middleware enforces it for
   every `/api/*` route (defence in depth on top of the per-route checks).
3. `auth.py`: accounts from env (break-glass) or DB; server-side sessions with idle timeout and revoke;
   password change. `security.py`: login/logout/me/password routes use them.
4. `admin.py`: `/api/admin/users` CRUD, reset, revoke; tenant-scoped; admins cannot lock themselves out.
5. Four-eyes check in `api.approve`.
6. Frontend: Admin page, change-password step, role labels, nav entry for admins.

### Risks & Follow-ups

Auth change: needs Ale's per-PR approval (AGENTS.md rule 9). Users live in the database: on the Render free
plan (ephemeral disk) they vanish on restart; the env admin stays as break-glass. OIDC + MFA stays SC-11.7.
Audit of sign-ins, views and downloads with a tamper check: SC-11.8 (next).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] #1 A viewer gets 403 on every write and on internal economics; an analyst gets 403 on /api/admin; a revoked or signed-out session is refused on the next request; an idle session expires
- [x] #2 The env admin still signs in (break-glass) and the existing security, tenant and role tests pass (regression guard)
- [x] #3 The person who uploaded or corrected a review cannot approve it; a second senior can

## Implementation Notes

- New: `pce/permissions.py` (table + `required(method, path)` + `active_role`), `pce/admin.py`
  (`/api/admin/users` list/create/patch, reset-password, sign-out), tables `user_account`, `auth_session`.
- `auth.py`: env/in-memory accounts first, then active DB users; server-side sessions (`sid` in the signed
  cookie), idle timeout `PCE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MIN` (30), `revoke`, `revoke_all`, `set_password`.
- `security.py`: middleware enforces the permission table and the first-sign-in password change;
  `/api/auth/logout` revokes; new `/api/auth/password`.
- `api.py`: `ctx` uses `active_role`; approval needs `report.approve` and passes the four-eyes check
  (`preparers` = document uploaders + fact reviewers), `PCE_FOUR_EYES=0` to disable.
- Frontend: `pages/Admin.tsx` (Team & access), change-password step in `Login.tsx`, nav entry for admins,
  admin-only accounts land on /admin, role switch renamed "Acting as" (feelens.spec.ts label updated).
